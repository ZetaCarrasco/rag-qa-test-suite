# Vacation Policy

Full-time employees receive 25 paid vacation days per calendar year. Vacation days accrue monthly, and up to 5 unused days can be carried over to the next year. Vacation requests must be submitted at least 14 days in advance through the HR portal and need manager approval. Employees may take up to 10 consecutive vacation days without special approval; longer periods require approval from the department director. Public holidays are not counted as vacation days.
