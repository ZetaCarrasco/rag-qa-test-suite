# Remote Work Policy

Lumen Works is remote-first. Employees may work from any country where the company has a legal entity, for up to 90 days per year outside their country of residence. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 UTC, and all employees must be reachable during these hours. Team meetings are recorded and shared in the knowledge base. A monthly home office allowance of 40 USD covers internet and electricity costs.
